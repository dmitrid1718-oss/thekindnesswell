No points. No followers. No promises. Just the act.

What we are doing

We are creating small disturbances of kindness and watching what happens.

Maybe kindness spreads through imitation, mood, attention, reciprocity, and ordinary human networks. Maybe intention and synchronicity play some stranger role. We do not need to decide before observing.

What we are not doing

We are not building a movement, selling anything, collecting donations, recruiting followers, keeping score of who is good, or promising that kindness will change the universe.

Maybe nothing unusual happens. That is completely acceptable. The kindness still happened.
